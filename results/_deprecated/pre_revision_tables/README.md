# Superseded pre-revision tables

These files were published in an earlier repository snapshot. They predate the
frozen single-generation analysis, validation-selected operating thresholds, and
the train-only augmentation correction used by the revised manuscript.

They are retained only to document the audit trail. Do not combine them with files
under `results/tables/yolov8s/` or `results/tables/fasterrcnn/`, and do not use them
to reproduce the revised paper tables.
